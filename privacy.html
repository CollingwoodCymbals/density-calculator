Privacy Policy

Cymbal Density Calculator

This app is made by Dave Collingwood (Collingwood Cymbals, Bristol, UK). This policy was last updated on 23 October 2026.

I don't collect any personal data. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

The cymbals you save to your favourites, and your settings, are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to me or anyone else. If you use "Back up favourites", the file is saved to your device, and what you do with it is up to you.

The app loads its pages from my website (collingwoodcymbals.github.io), which is hosted by GitHub. Like any website host, GitHub may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security. I don't have access to these logs. You can read GitHub's privacy statement at docs.github.com.

Links in the app to my main website (collingwoodcymbals.com) open in your browser and are covered by that website's own privacy policy.

The app is not aimed at children under 13.
