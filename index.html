Guide and disclaimer

What it works out

Density here means a cymbal's weight for each square inch of its surface. Enter the size and weight of a cymbal you like, and the calculator shows what another size would weigh at the same density.

It's a handy starting point when you're choosing, buying, making or lathing a cymbal to feel like one you already know.

What it doesn't consider

Two cymbals with the same density can still feel and sound very different. The calculator treats a cymbal as a flat disc, so it doesn't take account of:

• bell size and shape
• the profile and how high the bow is
• taper, where the metal is thicker or thinner
• lathing, partial lathing or none
• hammering, how much and what kind
• the alloy and how it was made

Use the numbers as a guide, alongside your ears and hands.

Disclaimer

This calculator is free and for guidance only. I can't promise any result from it, so please don't rely on it alone when buying, selling, making or changing a cymbal.
